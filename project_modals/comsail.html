CoMSAIL - JXWang Research Lab

Date: Aug 2025 – Present

I'm an undergraduate researcher studying optimal drone navigation through fluid currents using only imperfect existing flight data. The goal is to learn optimal navigation policies without ever flying a drone in a new environment by leveraging datasets of past suboptimal trajectories.

Some of my contributions:

  • Built a 2D Gymnasium environment with Taylor-Green vortex and homogeneous isotropic flow fields to simulate navigation under fluid dynamics.
  • Designed a reward function to mimic real-world drone energy costs.
  • Generated a 20,000-episode offline dataset of suboptimal trajectories for training.
  • Benchmarked 3 algorithms (Behavior Cloning, Decision Transformer, and MeanFlowQL) on the offline RL task.
